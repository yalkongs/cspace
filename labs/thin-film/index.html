What this explains

Light reflected from two interfaces interferes. Optical path length depends on thickness, refractive index and the angle inside the film. Fresnel coefficients set reflection amplitudes; the Airy expression includes repeated internal reflections. Different wavelengths interfere differently, producing structural colour without selective pigment absorption.

δ = 4π n d cos(θ_film) / λ; R = (R_s + R_p) / 2

Model and limits

A single smooth, lossless, non-dispersive film between air layers; unpolarized light; a 6500 K Planck illuminant. No substrate, absorption, roughness or multilayer stack. The preview is normalized to an incident perfect reflector, not automatically brightened for each thickness.
